I want to share here not an original story but what is probably my all time favorite ballbusting scene in a movie (pictures below).

It is from the 1979 French movie La guerre des Polices, with one of the hottest actresses at the time, Marlene Jobert, who plays Marie Garcin, a female police inspector who is after a dangerous criminal.

To follow a lead she enters an erotic movie theater. When she goes out to follow a person of interest, a stranger starts to give chase. On the theater screen, there is a close up of a beautiful woman moaning. There is where Marie turns around to confront the man. With no hesitation and precision, she shoots her foot up between the surprised man's legs, full force, like she would kick a soccer ball. 

She follows right away with "*take this! your turn to cum", and* turns back and leaves. The shot stays for long seconds on the man and the giant woman on the screen - she moans as he groans, several times, folding up more and more towards the fetal position. 

This superposition is simply a work of art. One of the hottest scenes in mainstream movies (mainstream at least for France in the 70s ;) )
