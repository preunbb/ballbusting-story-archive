# Overtime: Intro

The company consisted of several segmented areas for the technicians to work, a front counter for walk in business and an office area for the secretarial staff that handled the phones. It was your typical 9 to 5 workweek with a skeleton crew on the weekends. Most of us got the weekends off, but due to the fact that some of the companies we did business with were open on weekends, the newer secretaries had to take turns working alternate Saturdays.

I never worked on the weekend, that is until they hired this one girl named Jamie. She was 22yrs old, a petite 5 ft 2, blonde hair, blue eyes and had just about the cutest little feet you ever saw. She would almost always come into work with some type of sandal or easy to slip off shoes. She was not shy about taking her shoes off at her desk and would sit there working bare foot. I started to change my work routine so I would have more excuses to go into the front office, just so I could strike up conversation with her and check out her footwear for that day.

After a couple months we had developed a good working friendship.
