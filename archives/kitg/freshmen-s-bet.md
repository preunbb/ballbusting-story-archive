**Freshmen’s bet**

*Note: In my country it is common for universities and even high schools to held a summer camp for freshmen during the summer before their first year in their new school/collage/uni. The goal is to get the students know each other, make friends, have fun. Usually, seniors accompany them as mentors. They give advices, tell stories, etc.*

It was the middle of the freshman summer camp. Most of the soon to be university students enjoyed the slowly mildening temperature after the hot day. Naturally the beer tent had the biggest crowd.

Abby hopped on the bench next to Jack, and clinked her mug to his.

“Cheers! What’s up?” she asked.

“Well… about what?” he turned to her “Already overwhelmed with todays news on the majors. Too many good options.”

“So, what?” she shrugged “You will have years to decide.”

“But… I don’t have infinite learning power, I must start to think, which classes I should push, and which ones to just survive.” he said.

“I got you. You know, I have older siblings, and I can tell, that…”

She pushed his shoulder.

“Hey, are you listening?”

He shook himself.

“Yes, sorry… I was just wondering.” he answered finally.

“About what?”

He gestured towards another talking group.

“Lucy” he said.

“You like her, or what?” asked Abby a little puzzled.

“No… I mean” Jack thought for a second “She looks good, but not that. She is so… how should I put this a way you don’t misunderstand. She is too nice, too innocent.”

“Most people are nice here” looked Abby around “That’s the point of the whole summer camp.”

“I get it, I get it” gestured Jack heavily “But you know. She is just… the nicest. Too kind in her heart.”

“I still don’t get it” frowned Abby “You are also a pretty nice guy, you were gentle with everybody I’ve seen you with so far, so I don’t…”

“That’s different” interrupted Jack “Yes, I am a good guy. First of all, this is a time and place to make friends, not competition. Seconds of all, I am good and honest due to consideration.”

“Are you a fake nice guy or what?” Abby grew more and more puzzled.

“No. I am a good person, period. But because I know, that it benefits me, it benefits everybody. Okay, listen…”

He drank some of his beer, then went on.

“Let me explain. I am a good person. Because I think, if everyone acts good, is honest, trustworthy, everyone benefits. The more people you can trust, and the more people can trust you, the safer we are. The better the whole world is. It’s clear. I am a good person, and I want to be a good example. That’s the best for all people. But it’s my consideration. I am a selfish little prick in my soul. But being selfish, being evil, setting a bad precedent, it’s just harmful.”

He drank a little again.

“I prefer if everyone benefits from the system. That makes me want everybody to be a good person. Prisoner dilemma. Or so. If everyone benefits, I do as well. And that’s fine. I might tilt it a little, try gaining the most, but intend to harm nobody. I don’t steal, but when everybody is served, I might take the most. But I would never lose or risk my trustworthiness.”

“I get it now, I think.” Abby also drank a little “You are a good person, and overthink it.”

“But that’s not the point of my take. Look at Lucy. As I said, I will harm nobody, cross nobody, until the system works. But give me a videogame, a tabletop RPG, a scene on the stage. I can play the evil guy, until I know it’s a game, and nobody gets actually hurt. But not her. Lucy is the one, who reloads a videogame, because some random imaginary guy got sad. She could never utter a swear word on the stage out loud, because what if it disturbs somebody in the audience. She is too pure; she has a too bright soul.”

“I see, I see” nodded Abby “So, you think, she could not do any bad to anybody, even if she knows there are no consequences.”

“Yes.”

“You overrate it.” Abby shook her head. “She is nice, yes, most likely the nicest person in the whole camp. But cross her, piss her off, get her into a play, she is capable of anything.”

“Nah.” Jack waved aside.

“Wanna bet on it?” smiled Abby.

“Bet what on what?” opened Jack his eyes.

“Let’s say…” she thought for a second “Let’s see if she can do something bad, if she knows there would be no consequences… for anybody.”

She sipped some of her beer.

“If she does, I can ask something from you. If she really doesn’t, you can task me something. Like in a truth or dare."

“Like what?”

“I don’t know” Abby rolled her eyes “I am ready to bet dancing on this table in my thong bikini, if I was wrong.”

“Right, right” nodded Jack “I don’t have a thong. But since, I’m right…”

“Don’t worry, I can figure out something for you.” she smiled. “Ready?”

She stood up.

“Yes, I think” he nodded.

They approached Lucy. She was talking with two other freshmen.

“Hey, Lucy” started Abby “Sorry to interrupt, but I must admit, we couldn’t help but wonder. That you are the most innocent little girl here.”

“Oh, no…” rolled one of the other girls her eyes “Once again… about if you are still a virgin.”

“No, no, no.” shook Abby her head “I’m not that basic. Bleh!”

“I told her,” Started Jack “That you couldn’t hurt a fly. Even if you know, that there are no consequences.”

“I’m just trying to be nice to everybody” added Lucy. Her blonde hair and her glasses just made her look even more innocent. Even her voice sounded like a heartwarming tune.

“Yes, just as everybody else.” pointed out Jack “But… Let’s say: Could you hit me if I asked. Or do anything nasty?”

He took a few steps back.

“Okay, I will stand here. You can slap me, hit me… or, I don’t know, steal something from my pocket. I have my beer-money with me. I won’t do anything; nobody will ever face any negative consequence. Maybe I even like it. What if I am a submissive bitchboy, who likes being slapped or abused? What if I made a bet, and I can only gain, if I get hurt?”

He stood in a basic stance, his arm by his side.

“I even close my eyes. You can do anything nasty to me, just one little thing. I know you just can’t.”

Lucy seemed pretty hesitant. She stood there puzzled. She understood what Jack tried to point out. Maybe he was right. Then Abby whispered something in her ear.

Lucy stood there for a moment. Eyed Jack, as he stood there exposed. Then she stiffed herself, and swinged her right leg.

She kicked Jack in the groin. As a few gasps flew from the people standing around, he let out a weak wail, then he fell to his knees, and wheezed.

Almost everybody near them was shocked, just Abby was chuckling.

“I knew, you could do it” she said, and hugged Lucy.

Lucy’s face was uneasy. She seemed concerned.

“I hurt him” she muttered.

“Ah” gestured Abby with her hand “It’s nothing. Haven’t you ever kicked a guy in the nuts?”

Lucy thought for a moment. Then shook her head.

“Don’t worry” Abby calmed her “He will be fine in a few minutes, or hours. You cannot actually break his balls. It’s just their manly head, that intensifies the pain. He’s alright. Trust me, I’ve done this countless times.” She laughed.

“You did?” Lucy’s eyes widened.

“Yeah, why?” Abby shrugged “Best way to discipline your boy.” she giggled “I am not nearly as nice as you.”

They both smiled.

“Now, it has jumped into my mind.” spoke Abby again “I just won a bet.”

Lucy looked at her questioningly. Abby explained.

“He insisted, that you are nice to no limits. I told him, that you can do harm, in the proper conditions. Every lady can do.” she winked.

“Yes, but… now I feel sorry. Really.” added Lucy.

“I know, I know” went on Abby “That’s fine. But I was right. And now I can ask him to do something humiliating.”

She smirked.

“Jack” she raised her voice “Stand up for another!”

He looked up. His breath was now in order, but he was still on his knees, cupping his crotch.

“What?” he asked terrified.

“Stand up,” repeated Abby slowly “Put your hand aside. Then stand in a small spread.”

Jack started standing up, but he still covered his sensitive parts with his hands.

“Come on, Jack” teased Abby “You just told me about how trustworthy you must be.” she shook her head smugly. “You lost, and you must do this now.”

Jack rested his hands by his side. He gulped, and made an uneasy blink.

“Well” gestured Abby towards Jack as she turned to Lucy “Please, do the honors.”

Lucy slid her gaze from him to her.

“I can’t…” she said with a disturbed smile “You know, I… I just can’t.”

“Okay, alright.” nodded Abby “I see.”

She turned to Jack then glanced at Lucy.

“But I can” she added.

Just as she said it, she swinged her right leg, and rammed her feet into Jack’s crotch so fast, that it shocked everybody, who was still paying attention to them.

Jack cried out again, but he had no voice. He fell immediately to his knees, then laid on his side and curled up in the fetal position.

Lucy gasped, Abby laughed. In a few seconds, Jack started breathing again. In a way, which could be crying, laughing, or choking on water, they could not tell.

“Don’t worry” patted Abby Lucy’s back “He will be okay. In some minutes… a few hours… or a few days.” she giggled.

Lucy crouched by him.

“I’m sorry” she said with a concerned voice.

“Don’t be” said Abby “He earned it.”

She leaned to Lucy.

“And thank you.”

Lucy answered with a puzzled gaze. Abby stood back up.

“It was fun, thank you, Jack. Your drinks are on me this evening, you deserve it.”

She took a deep breath.

“Once you can finally get up” she added giggling.
