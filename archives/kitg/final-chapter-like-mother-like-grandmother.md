Final Chapter – Like mother, like grandmother

As Tina turned forty, she still was in good shape. She regularly moved all her limbs, and kept a healthy joyful lifestyle. She considered herself a lucky lady, as she could settle with her first boyfriend, and was a happily wed couple for almost two decades now. Isaac kept being a handsome, charming man, still doing sports regularly, and the only health risk he took was his wife crushing his nuts on an almost daily basis.

Tina was hanging around in the living room, as she heard the front door opening and closing. She recognized her daughter by her footsteps, but she wasn’t alone.

“Hi, Mom” her daughter said from the hall “I want to show you something.”

“Hi, Kelley” Tina called out from the living room “Go on!”

Kelley arrived into the room with boy about her age.

“Mom, this is Blake. My boyfriend for about three days now. I know him from school. Blake, she is my mother, Tina.”

Tina offered her hand. Blake shook it.

“Nice to meet you, Blake.”

“The pleasure is mine” he answered.

Tina gestured as she wanted to show around, yet she only took a half step, when Kelley and Blake attempted to follow. The young couple looked puzzled. Tina took the advantage of Blake’s opening by his halfway done step, and launched her feet into his groin. Blake lost all his air, widened his eyes, and bent over a little.

“Excuse us for a second” jabbered Tina, and as Blake nodded with agreement, she took her daughter’s hand and dragged her into the bedroom.

As she closed the door behind them, Kelley burst out.

“Mom, I already know about sex, don’t educate me!”

“I know, sweetie” Tina answered “I’ve seen your search history.”

Kelley blushed. Tina went on.

“But I bet you know quite little about relationships.”

Kelley nodded with acceptance.

“You need a guy with hard balls” Tina started.

“Mom… you were testing him?”

“Kind of.”

“Why do you think I haven’t done it already?” asked Kelley irritably.

Tina frowned amazed. She did not believe her ears.

“Mom, you cannot drop him with a clumsy kick. Catch this!” said Kelly, and went to the door.

Kelley opened the door. She looked at Blake and yelled.

“Urge!”

Blake obeyed like an animal would its tamer. He hopped, and arrived in a little spread, arm aside his body, looking at Kelley with anticipation.

Kelley started running toward him, and this was the run-up for an extremely impactful kick. Tina almost screamed in awe.

Blake fought to stand for two good seconds, breathless, then dropped on the floor and started panting pathetically.

“The keyword is ‘urge’” explained Kelley “Okay, not so creative, but meaningful. When I get the urge, you see. And floor again. That’s a two out of three thus far. The one which didn’t make it was a bad measure on the run-up messing up the hit, so zero-two for his berries.”

Tina was amused, and smiled with pride. She leaned to Kelley.

“He’s a keeper” she whispered.

“I know, mom” Kelley answered with normal voice.

A few seconds passed with Blake’s funny breath being the only sound.

“And, mom” spoke Kelley again “Can I… you know… stay at their place for the weekend?”

“For the whole weekend?” asked Tina surprised.

“Mom, we are way past the age of consent. And his mother is really nice. She likes me.”

“Fine” administered Tina “I hope you have protection.”

“We just got together.”

“And?” asked Tina frowning “You already have your tamer trick. Have condoms on the ready just in case.”

“Okay, okay” replied Kelly “If I promise to have some, can we go?”

“Yes” nodded her mother “It will be less crowded then. Elise is coming over for the weekend.”

“Elise?” asked Blake. He started to straighten up.

“My S-in-law wannabe” answered Kelley.

“Miles’s girlfriend” explained Tina.

Kelley and Blake went to her room and started packing her things for the weekend. Tina stayed in the living room, and overheard them speaking.

“My father arrives late on Fridays. I will introduce you next time” explained Kelley.

“Right. Will he also test me?” asked Blake.

“No, he won’t, don’t worry. He will most likely grab a beer or soda with you, and tell you everything about semiconductors, superconductors, classic video games, or fashion. Or all of those.” replied Kelley.

“Grand. He sounds like a cool guy.”

“The best” replied Kelley. Tina heard in her voice that she is smiling. “Best dad” added Kelley.

Tina couldn’t fall asleep that night. After rolling around for a few long minutes, she realized that she was thirsty. She looked at Isaac, who was slowly drifting off. He was curled up, instinctively covered his swollen balls with one hand, and pain was frozen on his face as he finally went off.

She smiled with joy as she cautiously got out of the bed. She went to the kitchen to get a glass of water. As she was heading back to the bedroom, she overheard her son from his room.

“Please stop! Let me just… ah! Ah! Let me rest for a minute” cried out Miles behind the door.

“Oh, man up!” heard Tina Elise’s voice “They aren’t even swollen yet.”

“Please” went on Miles “I… ah!” his scream went on with an increasing pitch, like his balls were intensely squeezed.

“Stop whining!” heard Tina Elise again.

The answer was muffled humming.

As Tina went on to her bedroom, she heard additional muffled groans. Then she heard Elise again.

“I see you like it even more than me.”

A short, muffled humming interrupted.

“Easy now, quick-shot. I will finish it, even if you go off, but it would hurt wa-ay more, so calm down.”

This was the last thing Tina overheard. She stepped into the bedroom, got on the bed, hugged her already sleeping husband, and soon dropped off with a proud smile on her face.
