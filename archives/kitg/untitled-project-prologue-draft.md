Victoria was never one to reminisce, not out of choice, but due to the intensity of her job. Doctors were hard to find, and somehow, a physical therapist like herself was assigned to handle the duties traditionally undertaken by a doctor in her sector. She had really always wanted to be a dancer, but she never had the swiftness or balance for it. But in times like these where you were either another mouth to feed or a skilled individual, people did what they could to get by.

  

While most of the injuries she dealt with were associated with those that accompanied labor, she would also assist birthing, and moving backwards in that direction, fertility.
