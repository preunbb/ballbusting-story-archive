Thought I'd have some fun...since I'm from the US, I used the Star Spangled Banner. Feel free to use another anthem or your stab with this one.

Oh say can you see

By the disco’s dim light

What so proudly she nailed

When he came unwantedly calling?

With her glossy high heels

Used to kick hard an high

Into orbit they flew

As she sent him a-sprawling

With her face in a glare

His balls burst in the air

And she laughed in the night

At the pain he must bear

Oh look at him curled up; his lesson well-learned

When you mess with a girl you get what you deserve
