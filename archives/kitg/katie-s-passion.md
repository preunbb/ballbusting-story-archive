**Katie’s Passion**

David decided to start visiting a karate training. He has never attended anything like that before. Maybe he was scared of the trainings; it is well known that the beginnings can be hard. But now he finally made up his mind, and went to the first training. The master was teaching and teaching, after a time they had to practice in couples. He had to practice with a girl.

Katie was not a beginner in karate. She was not a professional, but was experienced and liked to practice especially with boys. With them she was not that much expected to behave according to the rules. She liked to fight boys. It made her great pleasure to cause pain to the guys, but she did not have many possibilities to do so. Sometimes here on the trainings, but that’s not enough, she wanted more. David was a beginner. She was glad that she would fight (practice) with him, he did not seem too strong. They were in the corner a bit further from the others, and there was a wall where they could possibly hide from the sight of the others. Katie realized this...

So they started. Katie was always winning, she was more experienced. She sometimes hit David’s face and then said sorry. And then she saw the possibility… David left his hands too high and he left the space between his legs uncovered. Katie took her chance and kicked him in his balls with her beautiful barefoot. David lost control over his body, but did not fall. The force of the kicked pushed him close to the wall. No one was watching... so Katie finally fed her desires; she put her hands on his shoulders, pushed him to the wall and kneed him several times. What a great feeling for Katie, but painful for David. He lost his last energy to stay standing on his feet, and slowly he was falling down while Katie put her hand on his face, slightly buried her girlish nails in his cheeks, and covered his nose and mouth, so he could not breathe well... He fell on his knees, trying to breath. While he was on his knees Katie kicked his balls again with her beautiful toes burying between his legs. She hit his face slightly with her knee, just to show that she could break his nose or destroy his face, if she wanted, but David was already falling on the ground in pain right to Katie’s bare feet. His mouth ended on her feet. David was defenseless. Katie was thinking how to finish her victory. She put her toes in his mouth moving it from side to side. Finally she decided not have mercy. She did what she desired, lifted her foot higher and stomped his face, her sore left there on his face and stood on his head with her full weight. The smile of pleasure appeared on her face.
