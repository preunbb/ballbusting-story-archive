This was Jenna’s favorite part of her job. Getting to kick fellow college students in the crotch. She noticed the freshman were usually the ones to forego the custom fitting for the kick trials instead, since they were typically the most embarrassed and coy. Oh well, their loss, she thought. 

James went into the changing room to get back into his karate pants and top, and as he stepped out he asked, “Okay… so, where’s the first cup?”

Jenna smiled and explained, “Well, we need a baseline test, a control if you will, so we start off with a kick without protection.” The look on James’s face was so priceless, one of dread and disbelief, but he knew now this is what it would take to get a cup that would protect his junk during the rest of the semester. “Tell you what, for this first kick, I’ll go barefoot since you won’t be wearing anything. But after that, I need to keep my sneakers on so I don’t hurt my foot from the plastic of the cup.” She smiled again, knowing just how frustrating it must be for him to hear her talk about *her* pain, given what he was about to go through. She just couldn’t help but think about how amazing it was she was getting paid for all of this, too!

She brought him back to the training room and pointed to where he should stand. She stood a few feet in from of him and brought her foot up slowly to measure. He flinched every time she got close, and just when she was about to bring her foot all the way back, he jumped back and covered his crotch. “Wait! I’m sorry, I don’t think I can do this! It’s just that I know it’s coming, and I know I’ll jump out of the way.” She’d dealt with this before, so she had an idea.

”Okay, how about, just for this first kick, I’ll go from behind. That way you don’t see it coming.” He looked with consternation, but finally he just nodded, sighed, and turned around. Perfect, she thought. He was several inches taller than her, so she would easily be able to get under from behind and kick right into his groin. She lined up again, and without further ado, she planted her left foot, brought her right one back, and swung. WHAM! While she could feel his balls slam between the top of her foot and into his pelvis, she heard the satisfying smack of her foot into his scrotum. He stood on his tiptoes for just a moment before crumpling to the ground, grabbing his testes with both hands while kneeling, face on the mat of the floor, and groaning in a low tone. She said, “Wait here, I’ll grab the cups and jockstraps!” And she walked away, knowing that he would be down for at least a few minutes. 

By the time she came back in her sneakers and with the supplies, he was still on his knees on the ground, but he had at last taken his face off the floor. “All right, once you’re ready, we’ll start with the smallest cup we have. It’s the ‘Pee Wee’ size.”

James grabbed it from her and looked up with confusion. “Wait, why do I have to start with this? This is, like, for kids!”

”Well, James, maybe you’re lucky, I guess we’ll see, but all you boys grow up at different rates, and maybe some boys here are still kid-sized. Trust me, I’ve had some of your age only need this size.” With that, she sent him a quick look at his crotch, to which he shifted uncomfortably again and went beet red. “But, based on what I could momentarily feel, I’m sure you’re at least a *little* bigger than this size. But we won’t know till we try.” James went quickly to the changing room, at which point she could hear him make comments such as “no way this will fit.” She silently laughed to herself and waited patiently. He came out, walking with an awkwardly wide stance.

”Jenna, this absolutely is too small. Why do we even need to go through this test?” He was sounding a little testy, which she didn’t really appreciate, but she patiently answered, “Well, if you just swallowed your pride and dropped your drawers right now, I could properly fit you, as I explained before. But instead you’re too embarrassed, so either we do it my way, or you can go home with that cup you’re wearing right now.” Looks like he had a decision to make again.
