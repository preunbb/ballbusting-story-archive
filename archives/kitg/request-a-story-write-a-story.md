The other day, I messaged  dvhour with an idea for a story exchange, and he approved of the concept. This thread will hopefully facilitate that as outlined below:

Many of us have ideas for stories that we would love to see, but, at least in my view, writing a story for yourself doesn't really work. I would also guess that there are people who would love to contribute stories, but don't know where to begin. This post aims to address both of those problems while injecting fresh content into the site. It will work in the following way:

1. Comment below with a description of a story you would like to see. e.g. "Sister takes her little brother shopping for cups and teaches him why he needs one..." be as detailed as you like with your description.

2. As requests/descriptions come in, I will take each description and assign another person who commented on the thread to write that story. So for example, if John and Katy both comment story descriptions, Katy might write the story based on John's description and John would write a story based on Katy's description.

This should be a relatively simple system, but hopefully it will work out well. Some basic expectations to make sure that happens: Please do not comment a story description unless you intend to write the story assigned to you. This won't work if people make requests without being willing to fulfill other's requests. If you don't have time to participate in a reasonable timeframe, maybe hold off on participating in this exchange. If it goes well, hopefully more will happen in the future and you'll be able to participate in those. Please put some effort into your story, whether or not it's your cup of tea; everyone will be better off if everyone's story is higher quality.
