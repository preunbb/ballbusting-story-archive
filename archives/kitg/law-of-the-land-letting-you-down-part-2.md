Part 2

“You ready?” Jessica asks me, but before I even have a chance to answer, I get a kick to my balls. I let out a nice long “Ohhhhhhh” and hunch over before falling to my knees. Getting a kick to your bare balls from a capable ballbuster is a force to be reckoned with.

“Actually stay down there on your knees” Jessica commands. She then proceeds to kick me over and over again while kneeling, with my face slowly contorting from the pain that’s reverberating through my stomach. She starts kicking harder and harder until I can’t even stay on my knees and drop to all fours. She then comes around my back and starts kicking from behind. Those are so much worse, since you don’t see the kick coming. In those seconds between kicks, all you can think about is when the next kick is coming, and the seconds can feel like an eternity. It’s its own form of torture.

I finally collapse into the fetal position, prompting Jessica to taunt me, “Does that hurt? I’m sorry I don’t really know how bad it is, considering I’ll never know the feeling. I do know it’s fun to kick your balls.”

While I’m on the floor, I happen to look up at my wife, who has a grin on her face. “Wow I didn’t really expect that I’d like this so much” my wife says. “I could easily do this for you hun if you wanted. I’ll even top it off with something special” she says with a wink, which of course turns me on.

On that note, I slowly rise from the floor, like the phoenix from the ashes. Jessica taunts more, “So I see the man in you has finally come back out, and the fetal child has finally gone. Now I think I’ll try a little game.”

Peaking my interest, I ask, “What is the game?”

“I will think of a number between 1 and 20. If you get it wrong, I give you one kick and your wife gives you one slap.” My wife looks surprised, but then a smirk grows across her face.

“What happens if I win?” I ask, not knowing what to expect.

“Your wife will give your balls a nice massage and maybe even a release,” Jessica tells me, to my wife’s surprise. “You’re okay with that right?” Jessica asks my wife.

“Yes that sounds fun. I’ll be a team player,” my wife tells us.

“Let’s begin, shall we?” Jessica says as she positions herself to kick me.

“Wait, how do I know you’re telling the truth?” I ask impotently.

“You won’t”, Jessica says with a smirk.

“Okay fine. 1”, knowing it would not be right on the first try. Of course I feel the sting of Jessica’s kick, followed by a slap from Melissa, my wife. I proceed to go through all the numbers in order. I finally get to the number 20, as none of the other numbers seemed to be correct.

I get the kick to the balls and the slap, signifying I lost. I curl up in the fetal position, but I protest, “Hey, that’s all the numbers! How can they all be wrong?”  

“I never said I wouldn’t change the number each time you guessed”, Jessica says with a smirk.

Curled on the floor, I accept defeat. “Okay fine. You win. I cannot take anymore. I suppose this is payback for the issue from the office earlier”.

“Oh if you think this let’s you guys off the hook for that stuff from the office, you’re wrong. You should expect to see me again in that office. At least the next time I see you there, I’ll be a little happier picturing busting your balls over and over again”, Jessica says with a big smile.

“Ughhh I really feel bad for whoever you bust that is NOT into this”, I say.

“I did have fun here today. Hopefully now you know to just tell your wife about this stuff up front, and this wouldn’t happen. Or don’t, so that way I’ll be back here busting your balls again”, she says with a wink. “Have a great day, Melissa. I hope to see you very soon”.

On that note, she leaves.
