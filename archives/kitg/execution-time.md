](http://www.hotspotsz.com/images3/Amazon.jpg)It is execution time at the Amazon's camp. After the capture of the males, their fate has been decided by the queen and her committee: they will be executed. However, The Amazons do not think their captives deserve the honor to be executed with advanced weapons, or with weapons at all for that matter. Therefore, the soldiers have been ordered to use their bare hands or any parts of their bodies to execute the males.

Amanda has been selected to organize and coordinate the execution because she is one of the best martial artists among the group of female warriors. Soon Amanda realizes that not all warriors possess her skills, and that she has to settle on a fast and efficient technique that should work on every man, even performed by beginner fighters.

She knows acupuncture and vital points, and she quickly concludes that the most efficient way would involve a vicious attack on the testicles. From her fighting experience, this is by far the most sensitive part of that male's body, and beyond being extremely painful, and attack to the groin triggers all sorts of reaction in the male's body, as the balls concentrate the center of life energy. She knows that is might less efficient than with weapons, but the Queen's wish is not questionable, and she was confident that her warriors would bring the prisoners to the demise in a relatively short time. At least, Amanda knows she would.

As she prepared for her first experiment on a prisonner, she thought she should start by a series of hard front kicks in the balls. The aim is to provide enough shock. The kicks wold provide shock and enough pressure for the testicles to be hit and nicely squeezed at the same time. Repetition would ensure that both testicles would be hit. For some reason Amanda thought it is very important to hit both of them. She knew from experience that being hit at two different parts of the body provides more than double dose of shock and awe: if one ball was hit and one spared spared at first, it would already bring a great amount of shock; subsequents blow would ensure the other testicle is hit too, which would provide the most fantastic effect on her victim. At that point, Amanda was eager to start.
