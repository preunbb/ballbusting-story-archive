Bulging in his pants  
Watching the girl dance  
He paid his fee   
For a look-see  
Was he ever glad  
She didn't get mad  
At the peeping guy  
The little spy  
  
  
But then...  
  
  
Under his foot, the floor creaked  
And on him, guilt reeked  
She whirled around   
Knocking him to the ground  
With a well-placed slap  
He was in her trap  
She smirked down   
At her clown  
Chuckled, giggled at his owchie  
And was filled to the brim with glee.
