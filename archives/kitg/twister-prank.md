So my girlfriend Alexis and her other two friends Sarah and Lauren wanted to do a prank to my friend Colby one night while we were having a party. Whenever Colby would walk off to use the restroom or whatever it is he was doing, they'd come up to me and ask what a good idea would be to prank him.

  
"Well what kind of prank do you want to do to him?" I asked before making a few basic suggestions, "Saran wrap? A pie in the face? Something else?"

  
"We want something that will be really funny," Sarah replied as Alexis and Lauren agreed.

  
"Well what would be really funny to you?" I asked while contemplating if there was anything funnier then someone walking into saran wrap or getting hit in the face with a pie.

  
"I don't know," Lauren first responded.

  
"Ooh I know!" Sarah exclaimed, "What if we hit him in the nuts!"

  
"Oh yeah that's always hilarious!" Lauren agreed.

  
I was kind of in shock right at first because they just straight up wanted to be brutal about it. I thought that I should probably try and talk them out of doing this at first, but then I saw that look in their eyes that told me if I didn't go along with it, they were going to do it to me instead. Before I could respond to this suggestion Colby walked back into the room.

  
"Hey Colby," Alexis caught his attention, "Why don't you go out and get us some alcohol for the party!" She even handed him some cash to go purchase it. I figured this was a plan to get him out of the house so we could plan this prank out to perfection. He politely agreed and asked what they'd like and then took off. Colby is such a nice and polite guy and that made me wonder why they wanted to prank him so badly.

They'd been better off just straight up kicking him by surprise than setting it up as a prank, but then it came to me.

  
"I've got it!"

  
"What is it?" Sarah asked.

  
"Do you have Twister?" I asked Alexis. She went to go check in her younger sister's room and found the game. I figured out this would be the best way to do it. We'd make it seem like we were actually gonna play a game while drinking like some sort of challenge, then get him by surprise within the first few minutes.

  
"Alright girls, we're gonna talk Colby into playing this game. We're gonna act like this is a drinking game. I control the board and y'all will know when to do it. Which one of you wants to do it?" I continued.

  
"I'll do it," Sarah said, "I've always wanted to kick a guy right in the nuts!"

  
"Alright, " I said, "Y'all have to talk him into playing the game though."

  
Well about 20 minutes later, Colby returned with some beer. Everyone had a drink or two as we talked about life for a bit. Eventually Sarah brought up playing Twister, suggesting to Colby that it would be hilarious to play it while drinking because it would be harder to not fall. It took about 10 minutes or so and Colby finally agreed to do it.

  
They gave me the spinner and told me the be in control of it as the four of them would be competing against each other. I sat down on the sofa as they laid the mat down on the floor. Everyone took off their shoes to reveal their sock feet. All the girls were wearing some cute long and colorful socks.

  
"Y'all ready?" I asked, "Who's going first?"

  
Sarah agreed to go first. So I spun the spinner. But I wasn't going to go by what the spinner said. I was going to set this up quickly as possible. Lauren set up her phone on a shelf to record what was about to happen.

  
"Right foot yellow!" I said and Sarah placed her red socked foot on the second yellow dot from the edge of the mat. Then continued by having Alexis putting her left hand on green in which she used the corner directly behind Sarah. Lauren was then to place her left foot on red in which she used the corner across from Sarah. This was perfect because Colby was gonna have to get in front of Sarah to have room to move around.

  
My first step for Colby was having him set his left foot on green. And went back to Sarah to have her place her left foot on blue. I then followed by having the other two girls place their feet on dots directly beside of them.

  
"Alright Colby, we're back to you buddy," I said as I spun the spinner. I spun it pretty hard so it spun around for a fraction of a minute, "Right foot red!" This forced Colby to do a slight split across the board with this left foot on green and his right foot on red directly in front of Sarah who was standing perfectly within the board. I was sitting slightly out of Colby's sight, so he couldn't see me wink to let Sarah know this was when it was gonna happen. I asked her if she was ready for her turn and proceeded to spin the spinner.

  
"Right foot yellow once again!" I said and kind of laughed. Colby started to turn his head to look at me with an "are you serious" expression. But before he made the complete turn, BAM!
