Carly crouched behind a row of trash bins near the back exit of the Smoke & Steel Bar, her black hoodie pulled tight over her cap. Through the cracks between dumpster lids, she watched her target: Jake Delaney. Varsity lacrosse, ego like a wrecking ball, and an impressive track record of ghosting girls after one-night stands. Last week, he'd bragged on social media about tricking his date into footing the bill for dinner and Uber. The screenshots had landed in Carly’s inbox with the now-iconic subject line: #HeyCarlyHelp.

She adjusted her earbuds and zoomed in with her phone camera. Jake had just left the bar with two wingmen. Typical—always needed an entourage to feel tall. He was in that gray Carhartt jacket again, same one he'd worn in three of the posts the girl had tagged. She knew the gait, too.

Overconfident, wide-legged, like he thought the sidewalk belonged to him.

Carly slipped out of her hiding spot, careful to keep at a distance. The cool night air was damp with the tang of spilled beer and stale smoke. The trio crossed to the corner of King and Wolfe, laughing—probably at someone else’s expense.

She followed, silent, fluid. Her breath even. She could hear the thud of her hard rounded toe boots on the pavement. She always paid a lot of attention to the footwear she used for delivering justice to guys. Stilettos, high heels, even soccer cleats… she had used many shoes. For this job she settled on some hard rounded toe boots. They felt heavy as she moved quickly, an impediment now but they would make up for it later.

Ten blocks later, one of Jake’s friends peeled off. Another two blocks, and Jake himself ducked into an alley beside a vape shop—probably a shortcut to his overpriced condo. Perfect. Carly picked up her pace. She was running on instinct now, her blood singing with the anticipation of justice served cold.

She moved in.

The alley was lit dimly by a flickering red neon sign, giving it the sheen of a crime scene photo. Jake—still in that unmistakable gray Carhartt—was walking alone, scrolling through his phone.

Carly pulled the fake cash out, dropped it in his path, and slipped ahead. As predicted, he bent to pick it up.

Boom.

The toe of Carly’s hard black boot landed hard between his legs from behind. In an instant, his dangling gonads were mercilessly crushed against his pubic bone. These boots were paying dividends, thought Carly, as their weight and hardness effortlessly forced her target’s feet off the ground. All of this weight was now crushing his testicles against her hard boot. She brought her foot back down.

He looked up in confusion, clearly his body was in shock and still processing the trauma he received to his vital organs. She lunged. She grabbed his shoulder, spun him around, ready with the second act: a full force toe kick straight up between his legs.

CRUNCH!

  
The guy’s eyes bugged out, he screamed and stumbled back, his hands belatedly flying to his crotch in a vain attempt to protect himself. It was far too late.

Carly felt a wave of exhilaration overtake her. She has delivered two brutal kicks. She wouldn’t be surprised if this guy needed to go to the hospital. And then she froze.

Wrong face.

Instead of the scruffy stubble, the guy had a clean shaven baby face. He was maybe 21 or 22 she thought. His eyes were wide, confused, and oddly... kind. Not the smug smirk she was used to confronting. Not the look of a guy who thought women were toys.

“Oh... shit,” Carly muttered under her breath.

The guy was clutching his crotch, clearly trying to piece together what just happened. “What the hell?” He managed to squeak out, but it was barely comprehensible. Everything he said after that sounded like gibberish.

Carly knew what was happening, she had seen it happen to many guys she kicked. His brain was overloaded and he was going into shock. He was in for the worst moment of his life as the pain would start to build and build to intolerable levels.

She slowly backed away, her brain scrambling for an explanation she couldn’t find. “Sorry. Mistaken identity.”

His brows knitted. He again tried to speak but instead groans and screams came out.

Carly didn’t answer. She stood there, in shock herself. Her heart raced as she locked eyes with him and saw the crescendo of pain starting to build. She felt close and intimate with him. She had caused what he was now suffering, and she couldn’t move if she wanted to. But oddly, she didn’t want to.

She realized her heart was pounding not from guilt... but from the thrill. Her mind reeled, but her stomach didn’t churn. There was no dread. No shame.

Instead—she smiled.

She smiled as her innocent victim’s face started contorting into a mask of pain. He couldn’t speak but his wide blue eyes begged for mercy. None was possible, she thought. Nothing can stop the hell you’re about to experience, she thought about the guy. And that made her heart flutter with power and joy.

The wave of pain finally hit him and his eyes bugged out. His face was indescribable. She wished she could have had a picture. Then a moment later, a loud scream emanated from his mouth. He screamed again and again. His eyes locked on hers, he was screaming a pathetic cry at the top of his lungs.

Wow, she did this, she thought. She LOVED kicking guys in the balls more than anything else on Earth.

Her joy continued as he suddenly collapsed and started rolling around, like he was on fire or something. She had to hop out of the way, he was rolling everywhere. He was desperately grasping at his crotch, as if all this rolling and grasping would somehow make the pain go away.

That’s when she burst out laughing. Uncontrollably. “Ahhahahahaha” she couldn’t stop. He looked so pathetic! This was better than any comedy act she has ever seen.

After a minute of straight laughing she fell to her knees, still not able to stop. Her stomach was starting to hurt. “Not as much as his, no doubt, I wouldn’t be surprised if he throws up,” she thought to herself. As if on cue, he started dry heaving. “Ahahahha,” this made her laugh even harder.

This went on for what felt like an eternity. Him in hell and she in heaven - because she had put him into hell. And she loved it.

She heard a noise and saw someone approaching from the end of the alley. Instinct took over and she got up and ran. She ran a block and then blended into the crowd. She walked several more blocks. She finally found a park bench and sat down. She burst out laughing again, thinking about him flipping around like a dead fish.

She thought back to that moment of impact. His startled yelp, the sheer chaos, the disruption. Completely innocent. And yet...

She laughed again.

She wanted to do it again. Not because he deserved it. But because it was fun. And somehow, his complete innocence, his lack of deserving it… made it even more thrilling. She had completely dominated him for no other reason than she could - and she wanted to.

Carly leaned her head back against the cool brick wall and stared up at the sky. For the first time since starting her vigilante spree, she didn’t feel like she was righting wrongs.

She just felt alive.

And that scared her more than anything.

Was this a misstep?

Or was this who she was becoming?
