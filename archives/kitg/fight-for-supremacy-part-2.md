Kara and Dillon circled one another, each patiently waiting for the first move to be made. Kara stepped lightly on the balls of her feet, looking for an opening so she could take her boyfriend down to the carpet. She wasn't afraid of doing anything in her power to win. She wanted to hear Dillon admit he was wrong, she wanted to wipe that arrogant smirk off his face. 

Dillon didn't want to hurt his girlfriend but he wasn't about to lose to her so he knew he could not hold back. On top of that, her recent jest had caused his temper to flare up making him ready to unleash his anger. As such, Dillon decided to push the attack. He closed the distance between the two of them quickly and threw his fists out rapidly in attempt to get a fast victory.  

The flurry surprised Kara and forced her to turtle into a protective guard. She wasn't expecting Dillon to try and strike her! She thought they'd be rolling around grappling.  

Seeing Kara turtle fueled Dillon's offensive and he began to alternate between punching and kicking in search of an opening. But somehow Kara was able to avoid his strikes relatively unscathed. Dillon grunted as frustration began to build and he pushed forward intent on finishing this quickly.  

 Dillon pressed forward causing the corner of the couch to jab Kara in the back signaling that she was out of space. As confidence and fury raged through him, Dillon sent a wild punch that forced Kara to duck out of the way. The missed punch put Dillon off balance which gave Kara the opportunity to sweep his legs and knock him on his back.  

"Going a little hard aren't we honey?" Kara asked, her voice revealing some of the surprise that was brought on by Dillon's aggressive attacks.  

"You're the one who said there were no rules. I'm just teaching you a lesson." Dillon responded as Kara jumped on him, attempting to pin his arms down. 

The couple rolled about the floor, each one trying to get the upper hand and pin the other down. Eventually, Dillon was able to launch Kara off of him, sending her sprawling against the couch. As Dillon approached, Kara kicked out her foot which he managed to catch and grip with his strong hands. With a sharp twist, Dillon began to fold Kara's legs into a figure four leg lock. Kara's eyes widened as she began to feel an uncomfortable stretching sensation pulse down her entire leg.  

"Looks like you'll be giving up early," Dillon smirked as he pulled Kara's leg down, forcing her to cry out in pain. "Come babe, give up and before I hurt you more." 

Kara didn't respond, instead she pulled her leg back and slammed her foot into Dillon's chest. She breathed a sigh of relief as she slipped from his grip and rolled backwards into a defensive crouch.  

As Kara broke free Dillon scrambled to his feet, exhaling heavily in frustration. He was hoping that inflicting a little bit of pain would make Kara rethink her challenge and give up. It looked like he needed to do a little more to get her to give in. Seeing Kara crouched defensively, Dillon drove forward sending a heavy kick toward her.  

 Kara could tell that her resilience was beginning to frustrate Dillon. As a result, he wasn't being very discrete with his attacks. Getting out of that leg lock was a small victory but Kara knew she needed to bring the fight to Dillon. Up until this point she had mostly been on the defensive.  

Seeing the attack come in, Kara got her guard up in time and blocked the brunt of the damage from Dillon’s kick. The force of the kick knocked her up into the air sending her stumbling backwards. Kara regained her balance quickly and ducked away from a charging bear hug. Breathing heavily, she stepped back and watched Dillon to reveal his next move. Dillon transitioned from his attempted bear hug into a roundhouse kick, spinning to aim a kick towards Kara’s head. Kara saw the kick coming and deflected it before stepping around behind him. Her dodge forced Dillon into an unstable, half-split and she quickly saw an opportunity to go on the offensive. 

"How dare you try to kick me in the face!" Kara shouted before sending her foot up between Dillon’s legs. She felt her shin collide with the soft target causing a smile to curl up from the edges of her lips.  

Dillon's body lifted from the ground slightly before his abs tightened into a knot as his knees began to wobble. The pain emanated from his damaged manhood throughout his entire body, rendering every muscle useless. He curled forward in vain attempt to suppress the pain. Instead the pain increased and forced Dillon's legs to give out. He dropped to his knees and sputtered, “Not… Fair…” between shuttering breaths.  

“That’s the funny thing about guys. They can stack everything in their favour and no one bats an eye. But as soon as a girl works to balance the scales, it’s considered unfair. You’ve got big strong muscles, it’s only fair that I leverage your biggest weakness to counter them.” Kara giggled, clearly enjoying the moment. “Come on baby, you’re not going to give up after one little hit, are you?” She mocked, beckoning Dillon to stand.  

Dillon struggled to stand, trying his best to overcome the pain that coursed through his body. His legs trembled and his gut tightened as he pushed up into a low and unstable stance. Kara decided to push her advantage, not giving him any opportunity to recover from her blow. She threw herself at Dillon, knocking him to the floor and pinning him on his back. Hooking her ankles around his she stretched his legs out, smiling as Dillon grimaced in response.  

Dillon groaned as his groin muscles were strained by Kara's strong legs. He summoned as much of his leeched strength as he could to shove Kara's upper body off of him, weakening her stretch. Rolling his body, he ended up on top and was able to untangle his ankles from hers. He gasped a sigh of relief as the pressure on his groin muscles was released and he focused his efforts on pinning Kara's struggling arms.  

"Looks like my strength still trumps your dirty fighting, babe." Dillon chuckled as he finally succeeded in pinning Kara's arms. "Do you want to give up now?" He asked as he squeezed her wrists about her head, rendering them useless. His balls still ached but he was beginning to feel his strength return.  

Frustrated that she lost her advantage, Kara looked for a way out. She whimpered as Dillon taunted her, angry that might prove her wrong. "I'm not giving up," she said defiantly. It would take a lot more than a simple pin to get her to give in. She wriggled under Dillon's weight, trying to get any bit of herself free from him. Her struggles were unsuccessful and she decided for a different tactic; Kara brought her heels to her butt and bucked her hips swiftly, launching Dillon face first into the carpet.  

Dazed from the impact with the floor, Dillon rolled off of Kara and tried to regain his senses. Kara swiftly rolled out from under Dillon and got to her feet, grasping his ankles in an open V. She smiled as she held her foot over his manhood, giving him a moment to notice his plight before she sent her heel downwards.  

"No, wait!" Dillon shouted as his vision cleared and he momentarily saw how vulnerable he was. It was too late as Kara drove her heel into his unprotected balls, crushing them into his pelvis. His eyes bulged as he felt the wind crushed out of him. He let out an [audible](https://www.walmart.com/search/?query=audible "Shopping link added by SkimWords") groan and curled to his knees in to his chest, begging for the pain to subside. As much as he wanted to he couldn't make comment about the attack, fair or otherwise, as all he can do is cough and groan. 

Kara smiled at Dillon crumpled on the floor and stood with her hands on her hips. "Aww, did that hurt my big, strong man?" She cooed. Intent on winning the match as decidedly as possible, Kara wrapped her legs around Dillon’s neck in a reverse head scissors before bringing herself to the ground for extra leverage. She flexed her legs and squeezed her thighs to completely cut off his air supply. “Looks like someone might be about to lose to a girl,” She giggled, enjoying Dillon’s feeble struggles.  

Dillon refused to tap. Maybe it was his male ego or maybe he didn’t want to admit defeat at the hands of his girlfriend but whichever the case he continued to struggle. He pried at Kara’s legs trying to get as much air as possible while looking for an opportunity to break the hold.  

“The male ego. You should learn to admit when you’re defeated, it’d be less painful in the end.” Kara smirked, reaching her hand down to apply pressure to Dillon’s damaged jewels. “I can add a twist if you’d like,” she added mockingly as Dillon’s eyes widened and his body squirmed before he vigorously tapped out begging for the end of Kara’s torment.  

Kara laughed and gave Dillon a sharp squeeze before flashing a mischievous grin towards him. As she released his ball and untangled her legs from his neck, he groaned and rolled into the fetal position in vain attempt to numb the pain and hide his shame. Kara stood to celebrate her victory, placing a foot atop her defeated boyfriend.  

“Looks like girls can beat guys in a fight,” Kara blew a kiss down to Dillon. "It looks like your muscles can't make up for your vulnerability," she laughed before strutting out of the living, leaving Dillon on the floor curled in a ball.
