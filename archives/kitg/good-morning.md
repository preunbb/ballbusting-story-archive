She was on top, facing his balls  
Not even listening to his agonizing calls   
She pummeled, pounded and smacked them around  
And listened to his musical sound  
"Oh!  OH! OHH!" he cried, leaping with every one  
But this girly was far from done  
She loved to wake her love up this way  
Letting him know that this was a ballbusting day...
