A mischievous glint danced in her eyes as she gazed down at him, a devious smirk forming on her lips. *"Did you have a good nap?"* she playfully teased, the subtle sound of amusement lacing her voice.

His eyelids fluttered open lazily, revealing drowsy eyes that slowly focused on her face. Sensing his comfort, he nestled his head further into the gentle embrace of her lap. As he burrowed deeper, a sense of tranquil bliss washed over her; an inexplicable euphoria bloomed within as the realization dawned upon her - he wanted to stay there with her.

*"Yeah... I did,"* he murmured groggily in response to her question before mustering a yawn. An unintentional action followed suit: his hand reached down and discreetly adjusted himself for optimal comfort without any conscious deliberation. The act was instinctive, reflexive even.

Emma sat frozen in silence, wide-eyed and captivated by the sight before her. A rush of exhilaration coursed through every fiber of her being at what she had just witnessed. Her heart thudded against chest rapidly but not from apprehension this time - it was excitement that stirred within.

Her gaze lingered momentarily on his crotch before flicking back up to meet his face once more. *"Hey, I wanted to -"* She opened her mouth to speak softly when suddenly, their reverie was abruptly shattered by the sound of the front door swinging open.
