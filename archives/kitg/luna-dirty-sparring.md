The dojo was silent that afternoon, lit only by the golden light of the sunset filtering through the windows. In the center of the tatami, Luna watched her opponent with a smile that seemed sweet but concealed mischief in her eyes. Standing in front of her was a robust young man named Kaito, a black belt in karate, maintaining a solid and focused stance.

"Ready?" he asked, confident but respectful.

"Always," Luna replied softly, slightly tilting her head.

At Kaito's first move, a clean and direct kick aimed at her abdomen, Luna reacted with feline agility, dodging to the side and immediately responding with a sharp strike to his inner thigh. It wasn't exactly illegal, but it was certainly unusual for a friendly sparring session.

Kaito frowned slightly, surprised by Luna's initial aggressiveness, but said nothing. He launched another attack with a rapid series of punches, which Luna gracefully dodged, dancing around him, keeping her distance, and watching with bright eyes as the young man's frustration grew.

In a moment of carelessness, Kaito left his guard slightly open. Luna didn't hesitate: she quickly extended her fingers, firm and precise, aiming directly at his eyes with a short but determined motion. Her fingers brushed with enough force to cause a sharp pain, making him recoil with a surprised grunt, instinctively bringing his hands to his face to protect himself.

"Luna, be careful!" he protested, rubbing his eyes, which had reddened and watered visibly from the sudden attack.

She only laughed, covering her mouth with her hand as if it had been an innocent mistake.

The fight continued, and although Kaito increased the intensity, Luna seemed to anticipate every move. A quick strike to his neck, a well-timed knee to his hip, and each time her tactics grew more painful and less orthodox.

Kaito, though visibly hurt, kept getting up and adjusting his stance, showing admirable tenacity and resilience. Luna, far from stopping, only escalated her cunning. In a close-range exchange, her knees found Kaito's abdomen with such precision that it knocked him to the ground, gasping for air.

"Do you want to continue?" Luna asked, smiling with feigned sweetness, her eyes gleaming with satisfaction at seeing him panting.

Kaito, proud and determined, firmly nodded as he slowly got to his feet. But before he could fully stand, Luna prepared an even more ruthless attack: with cold, calculated precision, she launched a direct kick to the young man's groin, striking hard with the top of her foot, right at the most vulnerable spot. The blow was sharp, relentless, specifically designed to completely buckle and disorient her opponent. This time, the boy collapsed onto the tatami, unable to hide the intense pain.

"I think that's enough for today," she said cheerfully, offering her hand to help him up. Her mischievous smile remained intact, thoroughly enjoying the chaos and confusion her unique style had caused.

Kaito looked up at her from the floor, still gasping, a mixture of admiration, frustration, and respect on his face.

"You... definitely don't fight clean."

Luna slowly leaned closer to him, whispering with a mischievous smile:

"Who said I would?"
